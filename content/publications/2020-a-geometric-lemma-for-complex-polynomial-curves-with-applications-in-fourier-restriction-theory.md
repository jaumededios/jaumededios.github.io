## Abstract

The aim of this paper is to prove a uniform Fourier restriction estimate for certain 2−dimensional surfaces in R2n. These surfaces are the image of complex polynomial curves, equipped with the complex equivalent to the affine arclength measure. This result is a complex-polynomial counterpart to a previous result by Stovall [Sto16] in the real setting. As a means to prove this theorem we provide an alternative proof of a geometric inequality by Dendrinos and Wright [DW10] that extends the result to complex polynomials.
