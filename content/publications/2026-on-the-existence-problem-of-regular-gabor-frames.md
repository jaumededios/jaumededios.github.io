## Abstract

For every dimension d>1, we establish explicit criteria on lattices Λ⊂ℝ2d with density D(Λ)>1 such that no function with a continuous Zak transform generates a Gabor frame along Λ. In particular, this gives a negative answer to the existence problem of Gabor frames with window functions in the Schwartz space, the Feichtinger algebra, and the Fourier-invariant Wiener space. Our result is based on a characterization of when a collection of quasiperiodic functions admits a common zero, which may be of independent interest. We also include a formalization of our main result in Lean 4.
