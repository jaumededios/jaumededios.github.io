L2-Stability for STFT phase retrieval

Authors: Susanna Bertolini, Jaume de Dios Pont, Ben Pineau, Mitchell A. Taylor, João P. G. Ramos

Type: Preprint (2026)

arXiv:2605.20527

Abstract

We prove that the short-time Fourier transform with Gaussian window performs L2-local stable phase retrieval at the constant function. The proof involved significant interplay between mathematicians and LLMs. An autoformalization in Lean 4 of an extension of our result to L2-local stable phase retrieval for all Hermite windows and all elements in the finite span of the canonical basis vectors is also presented
